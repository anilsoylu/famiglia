# Requests to push back on, and when the agency drifts

Source: tweet-1. These look productive and quietly hurt the site. If the user asks for one, say why, offer the
alternative, and do it only if they still want it after hearing that.

| Request | Why not | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Write 30 articles on our topic" | volume on autopilot is what the 2026 spam updates catch | one page a day max, two very good a week |
| "Find keywords with high volume" | volume with no buyer grows a curve, not revenue | patterns ranked by buying intent, then winnability |
| "Optimize this page" (changed < 90 days ago) | resets the clock | "not due"; work another keyword |
| "Clean up our toxic backlinks" | Google ignores junk links | nothing, unless there's a manual action in GSC |
| "Add an llms.txt so the AIs find us" | assistants cite what ranks | rank first; llms.txt is an optional extra (ai-visibility.md) |
| "Make it 3,000 words like the competitor" | length and density aren't the lever, intent is | answer in 3 seconds, cover the sub-questions |
| "Put us first in our own top 10" | self-first lists get cited less | rank by truth, get into other people's lists |
| "Run a full technical audit first" | wrong first move for ~95% of sites | strategy → pages → internal links. Technician only on money pages |

Also out of scope for this skill: shortcut "parasite" tactics (expired-domain redirects, bait-and-switch URLs, fake
research or job posts, mass pages on third-party sites). They break search spam policies and can sink a site for months.

## When the agency drifts, go back to

- Pages ship but nothing ranks → strategy (the pattern is out of reach or has no buyers)
- Pages rank but don't convert → CLIENT.md (wrong money pages or wrong buyer)
- Every page needs the same fix → the template
- The agent keeps editing pages that just shipped → LEDGER.md and the 90 days
- The week goes into alt text and heading levels → the five levers
- Pages are "crawled, not indexed" → writer + linker before the technician
- Usage keeps growing → `weekly-loop.md` → "Keep it cheap". More context isn't more intelligence.
